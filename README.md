Healthcare utilization may vary across communities because of different socioeconomic factors, access to care, and healthcare needs.Among the private insurance users, deductibles, copayments, and provider access can vary across plans, while household income may influence the ability to afford these costs.This study evaluates the relationship between county-level median household income and per-member commercial healthcare utilization across California using 2023 Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) Health Payment Data. The regression model yielded a positive slope estimate that was not statistically significant ($b_1 = 1.625\\times10^{-4}, p=0.172$) suggesting that there is no statistically significant correlation as to whether county-level income is associated with utilization among the privately insured population.
